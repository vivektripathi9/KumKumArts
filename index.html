Aravalli Farm Art Retreat · 8–9 August 2026

What is the Aravalli Farm Art Retreat all about?

The Aravalli Farm Art Retreat with Kumkum Arts is a two-day escape at Kivara Homes in the Aravalli Hills, Alwar — slowing down, walking lush farms, picking fresh fruit, meeting the cows, and creating art in a beautiful farm setting. Through a guided, beginner-friendly flow, you try pottery, Jenga block painting, and wine glass painting, and leave with memories and work you made with your own hands.

Wondering if you have to be an artist?

You don’t need a portfolio. This retreat is for beginners and practising artists alike — creative prompts, experimental activities, and zero judgement. Show up curious; we handle the rest.

Think of it as a meaningful weekend away from routine: pottery, a morning hike + picnic through Aravalli trails, wine glass Sip & Paint, and bonfire-style evenings of games, Jenga block painting, storytelling, and late-night chats.

What you’ll step into

  • Pottery workshop on Day 1 and Jenga block painting in the evening
  • Aravalli trail hike + picnic and wine glass Sip & Paint on Day 2
  • Double-sharing villa stay at Kivara Homes, meals as listed, Delhi transport included — see the current event page (from ₹8,999 early bird)

Note: You don’t need any background in art. It’s about flowing between clay, colour, and conversation - curiosity is enough.
