Let’s Paint · Manali · May 29–31, 2026

What is the Manali Art Retreat all about?

The Manali Art Retreat with Kumkum Arts is a long-weekend escape that blends making art with mountain ease - three days in Manali where creative sessions, time outside, and shared meals sit side by side. Through a guided, beginner-friendly flow, you can slow down in the hills, pick up new skills, and leave with work you shaped from start to finish.

Wondering if you have to be an artist?

Here’s the good news: you already are one in the way that matters here. You don’t need to master a craft before you arrive. Let’s Paint is built for conversation, play, and first tries - a safe space to explore colour and clay, meet people who get it, and share stories without pressure.

Think of it less like a graded class and more like a reset in good company: gentle structure, mentors who won’t rush you, and room to breathe between pottery, canvas, and mountain air.

What you’ll step into

  • Hand-building pottery and a paint-your-pottery session to finish what you made in clay
  • Live painting outdoors - our art-picnic day with nature, music, and slow sunset moments
  • Café-style meetups, group activities & games, and slow mornings before you head home
  • Shared stay in Manali, breakfast & dinner on us, and Volvo travel as spelled out on the current event page (from ₹9,500 shared stay)

Note: You don’t need any background in art. It’s about flowing between clay, colour, and conversation - curiosity is enough.

Where Will We Stay?

The May batch beds down in Manali in a modest mountain lodge: double or triple rooms (whatever you pick at signup), straightforward comfort, and enough quiet that studio days don’t bleed into sleepless nights. Breakfast and dinner sit inside the retreat fee; lunch stays off the bill on purpose so you can wander into town for thukpa or coffee when we break. Nothing here is meant to read like a brochure, and if you want the straight list of what’s included, what isn’t, and how rooms work, it’s all written the same way on our stay page and on the Manali event page.
