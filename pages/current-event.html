What is an art retreat?

Escape into art, farms & slow living

“Disconnect from routine. Reconnect with colour, clay, and people who speak the same quiet language of curiosity.”

Join us for a 2-day immersive art retreat at Kivara Homes in the Aravalli Hills, Alwar — walking lush farms, picking fresh fruit from the trees, meeting the cows, enjoying peaceful sunsets, and creating together in beautiful villas.

No prior art experience required. Beginners and practising artists are all welcome.

  • ◇ Pottery, Jenga block painting & wine glass painting sessions
  • ◇ Farm walks, fresh fruit picking, Aravalli trail hike & picnic in nature
  • ◇ Bonfire-style evenings — card games, conversations & laughter — only 12 spots

Your weekend, mapped

What to expect — day by day

Pottery, ridgelines, paint under open sky — then the kind of laughter that carries across the farm.

Day 1

Slow down & create

7:00 AM pickup from Delhi (Kashmiri Gate). Reach Kivara Homes by around 10:00 AM. Welcome snacks, fresh fruits & tea, check-in, and an introduction circle. Explore the farms, then a pottery workshop and lunch. Free time to relax. Evening farm walk — meet the cows and hand-pick fresh fruits. Dinner, then a bonfire-style jamming session with conversations, card games, and Jenga block painting.

Day 2

Nature, art & picnic

Breakfast, then a morning hike through the Aravalli trails. Picnic amidst nature, followed by a Sip & Paint session — paint your own wine glass while enjoying the beautiful surroundings. Lunch, then wrap up, pack your memories, and depart around 6:30 PM. Expected arrival back in Delhi around midnight.
