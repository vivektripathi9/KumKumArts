What is an art retreat?

Escape into art, mountains & slow living

“Disconnect from routine. Reconnect with colour, clay, and people who speak the same quiet language of curiosity.”

Join us for a 3-day immersive art retreat in Jibhi — creating together, exploring nature, sharing stories, and building real friendships with fellow creatives at Odin Hostel.

No prior art experience required. Beginners and practising artists are all welcome.

  • ◇ Pottery & painting sessions, creative prompts & experimental play
  • ◇ Scenic hike + picnic-style live painting in the mountains
  • ◇ Card games, storytelling, late-night chats — limited spots

Your long weekend, mapped

What to expect — day by day

Pottery, ridgelines, paint under open sky — then the kind of laughter that carries down the valley.

Day 1

Pottery workshop

Arrival and check-in, then a hand-building pottery session. Ice-breakers and creative warm-ups, art discussions and community time, and evening games, conversations, and a cozy mountain night.

Day 2

Hike + live painting picnic

Morning breakfast, then a scenic hike around Jibhi. Picnic-style live painting in the mountains, creative exploration outdoors, and back for evening art, discussions, card games, and plenty of laughter.

Day 3

Pottery finishing session

Complete and decorate the pieces you started on Day 1, then a reflection circle and group activities. Farewell and departure — take home what you made and the quiet confidence that comes with it.
