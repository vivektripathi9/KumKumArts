What is an art retreat?

Escape into art, mountains & slow living

“Show up tired from the city. Leave with paint on your hands and room in your chest.”

Join us for a cozy creative getaway in Manali — three days of making art, wandering pine trails, meeting people who get it, and finally exhaling.

No portfolio required. Curiosity is your only ticket.

  • ◇ Clay, canvas, and conversation — guided, not graded
  • ◇ Altitude: views. Attitude: zero pressure
  • ◇ Small circle, big heart — you will not get lost in the crowd

Your long weekend, mapped

What to expect — day by day

Make messes. Watch sunsets. Laugh too loud at dinner. Repeat.

Day 1

Arrival & hand-building pottery

We begin the retreat with a cozy café meetup, getting to know each other through conversations, games, and calming hands-on art — a hand-building pottery session with clay modelling.

Day 2

Art picnic in the mountains

An afternoon of live painting surrounded by nature, followed by a small hike, picnic setup, music, and slow sunset moments.

Day 3

Slow mornings & paint your clay pieces

Ending the Retreat by painting our handmade pottery pieces, sharing one last breakfast together, clicking photos, and taking home memories (and art!) made with our own hands.
