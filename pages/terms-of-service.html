Terms of Service

Last updated: April 15, 2026

1. Acceptance of terms

By booking, joining, or using any Kumkum Arts retreat service, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not proceed with a booking.

2. Retreat bookings and payment

Bookings are confirmed only after receiving the required advance payment and a written confirmation from our team.

All quoted prices are in INR and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

3. Cancellation and refund policy

Cancellation 21+ days before retreat start: 80% refund.

Cancellation 8-20 days before retreat start: 50% refund.

Cancellation within 7 days: no refund. You may request a one-time transfer to a future retreat date, subject to availability.

4. Participant responsibility

Participants are responsible for sharing accurate health details, emergency contact information, and any special accessibility requirements before arrival.

We reserve the right to remove any participant whose behavior threatens safety, wellbeing, or the retreat experience of others.

5. Travel and accommodation

Participants are responsible for travel to and from the retreat location unless a transport add-on is explicitly purchased.

Shared accommodation is standard unless private upgrade options are listed and confirmed in writing.

6. Media and content usage

Photos and videos may be captured during the retreat for promotional use on website and social channels.

If you do not wish to appear in public media, inform our team in writing before or at check-in.

7. Liability limitation

Kumkum Arts is not liable for indirect loss, missed travel, personal valuables, or events outside our reasonable control including weather, road closures, or government restrictions.
