Art, Create & Connect — Shimla Art Retreat

3 Days of Creative Bliss in the Himalayas

Dates: 11–13th September
Location: Vohra Mansion, Shimla

Three days of art, creativity, slow living and meaningful experiences, tucked away in the mountains of Shimla. Come spend a few beautiful days creating, exploring and connecting with yourself and others.

Note: This is a small, intimate retreat with only 8 spots available.

About the Retreat

This retreat is an experience, not just a workshop. We combine art, creativity, good food, wellness, and nature to foster a sense of community and slow living. It's a space to pause, breathe, and reconnect with your inner artist.

Pricing

  • ₹18,500 per person (Double Sharing)
  • ₹17,500 per person (Couple Offer)

Our Activities

Make Your Own Tiramisu Jar

Enjoy a fun, hands-on dessert-making experience.

Hand-Building Pottery

A relaxed session creating your own piece. No experience required.

Ocean Texture Painting

Experiment with textures, colours, and painting techniques.

Paint Your Own Jenga

Personalise your own Jenga set, followed by a fun group game.

Yoga & Wellness

A calming session to slow down, relax, and reconnect.

Museum / Gallery Walk

A curated walk exploring local creative spaces.

Food & Meals

Delicious food is central to our retreat. All meals are prepared at the villa by our private chef, and we manage all grocery sourcing so you can simply enjoy fresh, wholesome meals together.

Your Mountain Home

We will stay together in a private villa, creating a comfortable and intimate retreat environment. Your stay includes private accommodation, meals, and beautiful shared spaces to relax and connect.

What’s Included

Your retreat fee covers everything you need for a restorative experience:

  • Accommodation: Comfortable stay in our private villa
  • Food & Meals: All meals prepared by our private chef
  • Art Materials: All supplies provided for your creative projects
  • All Retreat Activities: Pottery, painting, Tiramisu making, and more
  • Wellness: Guided yoga and wellness session
  • Experiences: Curated museum and gallery walk
  • Transportation: Provided for all retreat activities where applicable
